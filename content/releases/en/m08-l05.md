# M08-L05: Lab: aggregate logs by service

> **Guiding question:** Why must a duplicate service update totals rather than replace a prior row?

## Learning objectives

- Use HashMap::entry to initialize missing keys and accumulate subsequent events.

- Preserve borrowed service names where the output's lifetime is tied to input, rather than cloning by default.

- Collect and sort the results explicitly for deterministic output even when the source map iteration order varies.

## Practice transfer: Accumulate before sorting

**Predict:** Does inserting api:10, web:2, api:5 produce api's total of 15 automatically?

**Implement:** Repair the verified M08 starter's entry accumulation and explicitly sort the final rows.

**Counterexample and boundaries:** Test duplicates, empty logs and Unicode service names; a map size assertion alone cannot detect broken counts.
