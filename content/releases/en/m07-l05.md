## Reason through a counterexample: Swap parser implementations

**Problem:** Shared traits do not guarantee correct delimiter behavior.

**Principle and deduction:** Replaceable parsers share an input/output and error contract; they do not have to share the same delimiter logic.

**Example and evidence:** Verify with the existing example or lab: Repeated delimiters and empty fields are tested.

**Counterexample:** A colon parser that uses the equals rule, or silently ignores malformed fields, may implement a trait and still violate behavior.

**Independent exercise:** Run the same contract tests against two parser implementations for valid, absent and malformed separators, swapping them without changing callers.

**Review:** A type-correct trait implementation is not proof of semantic interchangeability.
